# Peculiar Pioneers — Full Website Language Review

September 14, 2026 · Proposed copy for Design Preview 02

This review checks consistency with Ellen G. White’s **The Great Controversy (1911), chapters 18–42**, and **all thirteen chapters of Steps to Christ**. It covers the current interactive preview, available live-page copy, and the supplied sanctuary lesson source. The corrections are prepared in a new preview; the live site and original repository have not been changed.

## Main finding

The core sanctuary and Sabbath teachings are broadly consistent with the requested texts. The most useful revisions make the gospel more explicit: Christ draws us to repentance; His righteousness is our acceptance before God; His Spirit changes the heart; obedience and service are the fruit of living faith. About Us was incomplete in places, rather than generally false.

The Evidence page needs a stronger distinction between biblical/EGW teaching and historical documentation. Its old claims that a selected list is “comprehensive,” demonstrates an “unmistakable” increase, and proves precise prediction of present headlines go beyond what the list establishes. Its color coding and year-by-year layout can remain.

**This is not a blanket accuracy certification of every item on the website.** There are 570 original event entries; only the corrections identified below have been independently checked against the cited historical records. Forty-six of the 48 timeline quotation placements cite EGW works outside the requested two books and still require verification. Video audio was not reviewed.

## Important teaching decisions

- **Grace and obedience:** The old site mentioned righteousness by faith but did not explain it fully. The revision makes acceptance through Christ and obedience through His renewing power explicit. [Steps to Christ, 59–63](https://www.ellenwhite.info/books/bk-sc-07.htm); [Steps to Christ, 68–72](https://www.ellenwhite.info/books/bk-sc-08.htm).
- **Repentance:** We should not tell people to reform themselves before coming to Christ. He draws them and gives repentance, which includes turning from sin. [Steps to Christ, 23–26, 31](https://www.ellenwhite.info/books/bk-sc-03.htm).
- **Judgment and assurance:** Keep 1844 and the investigative judgment, while explaining Christ’s advocacy and the merits of His sacrifice. Do not announce a revealed date when the judgment of the living began. [The Great Controversy, 482.4–485.2; 490.1](https://www.ellenwhite.info/books/ellen-g-white-book-great-controversy-gc-28.htm).
- **Three angels:** Include the everlasting gospel and the announcement of the judgment hour, as well as worship of the Creator, Babylon’s fall, and the warning against false worship. Distinguish Revelation 18’s call to come out from a direct quotation of Revelation 14:8. [The Great Controversy, 355.1–355.3](https://www.ellenwhite.info/books/ellen-g-white-book-great-controversy-gc-20.htm); [The Great Controversy, 603.1–605.2](https://www.ellenwhite.info/books/ellen-g-white-book-great-controversy-gc-38.htm).
- **Sabbath and the mark:** The final issue is an informed choice after the truth has been made plain under enforced worship. Present Sunday observance alone does not warrant saying that a person has received the mark. [The Great Controversy, 449.1–450.1](https://www.ellenwhite.info/books/ellen-g-white-book-great-controversy-gc-25.htm); [The Great Controversy, 604.3–605.2](https://www.ellenwhite.info/books/ellen-g-white-book-great-controversy-gc-38.htm).
- **Calamities:** GC 589.3 does warn of increasing calamities. The error is claiming this selected event list itself proves the trend or that each event was individually predicted. GC 590.1 describes false blame against God’s people; the passage should not become a way of blaming disaster victims. [The Great Controversy, 589.1–590.1](https://www.ellenwhite.info/books/ellen-g-white-book-great-controversy-gc-36.htm).
- **Final plagues:** Their placement follows the close of Christ’s intercession. Do not identify every modern disaster as one of those plagues. [The Great Controversy, 613.2–614.1; 627.3](https://www.ellenwhite.info/books/ellen-g-white-book-great-controversy-gc-39.htm).
- **Death and resurrection:** The general resurrection of the wicked follows the thousand years. The qualifier “general” avoids overlooking the special resurrection discussed in GC 637.1. [The Great Controversy, 637.1](https://www.ellenwhite.info/books/ellen-g-white-book-great-controversy-gc-40.htm); [The Great Controversy, 660.4–661.2](https://www.ellenwhite.info/books/ellen-g-white-book-great-controversy-gc-41.htm).
- **Practical godliness:** Health and modesty belong within a wider life of love, honesty, temperance, service, and character change. Health practices do not earn salvation. [The Great Controversy, 469–475](https://www.ellenwhite.info/books/ellen-g-white-book-great-controversy-gc-27.htm); [Steps to Christ, 77–83](https://www.ellenwhite.info/books/bk-sc-09.htm).
- **The ending is hope:** Warnings should lead to faithfulness and compassion, with Christ’s victory and the new earth clearly in view. [The Great Controversy, 674–678](https://www.ellenwhite.info/books/ellen-g-white-book-great-controversy-gc-42.htm); [Steps to Christ, 115–126](https://www.ellenwhite.info/books/bk-sc-13.htm).

## About Us — complete proposed wording

Peculiar Pioneers is a Seventh-day Adventist ministry sharing the everlasting gospel and the three angels’ messages of Revelation 14. We invite people to know Jesus Christ, study His Word, and prepare for His return through repentance and faith in Him.

The everlasting gospel reveals God’s love in giving His Son to save us from sin. Jesus died for us, rose again, and ministers as our High Priest in the heavenly sanctuary. We depend on His righteousness for acceptance with God and on His Spirit to renew our hearts and enable us to obey His commandments.

The word ‘peculiar’ in our name comes from 1 Peter 2:9, where God’s people are called ‘a peculiar people’ and are to show forth His praises. The pioneers of the Advent movement remind us to search the Scriptures prayerfully, comparing passage with passage. We seek to study, live, and share the truths of God’s Word with humility and love.

Through sermons, Bible studies, and podcasts, we explain the sanctuary, the Sabbath, the three angels’ messages, and the hope of Christ’s return. We also encourage confession and the forsaking of sin, daily prayer, healthful living, and practical care for others. While Christ intercedes for us, His invitation to repent and receive His grace remains open; our purpose is to help people respond to Him now.


**Mission**

To proclaim the everlasting gospel and the three angels’ messages: calling people to repentance and faith in Jesus, announcing the hour of God’s judgment, honoring the Creator and His commandments, and sharing His call to leave Babylon and be ready for Christ’s return.

**Values**

**Scripture First** — The Bible is our rule of faith and practice. We study it prayerfully and test every teaching by its testimony.

**Present Truth** — We share the everlasting gospel with the sanctuary, the Sabbath, the judgment, and the three angels’ messages as we look for Christ’s return.

**Christ-Centered** — Jesus is our Saviour, righteousness, and High Priest. We depend on His grace for forgiveness and for a life of loving obedience.

**Practical Godliness** — Faith in Christ bears fruit in honesty, kindness, modesty, temperance, and service. We seek to honor God in our thoughts, words, dress, health, and conduct.


The existing “Our vision for serving families” section remains. It describes hopes and future work; it does not claim that assistance programs, counseling, medical care, or financial grants are currently operating.

## Items that remain unresolved

1. **Historical timeline:** Most events lack item-specific primary-source links. The companion CSV lists all 570 original records and their status. The priority list below identifies wording/date/count problems for follow-up; those flags are not asserted as newly verified corrections. I would complete that source pass before presenting the chronology as historical “evidence.”

2. **Outside-corpus EGW quotations:** Two timeline placements from GC 589 were checked; 46 cite other books, manuscripts, or compilations. They remain attributed as inherited text, visibly marked as awaiting verification. Being outside this review does not make a quotation false. It means the requested books cannot certify it.

3. **The Godhead statement:** The existing three-co-eternal-Persons formulation is retained as the ministry’s faith statement. The full formula is not explicitly developed in the requested source range, so I have not attached a misleading GC/SC proof citation or silently rewritten the ministry’s doctrine. The KJV Scripture references remain. A fuller EGW-source verification requires additional writings.

4. **EGW’s prophetic ministry:** The revised “Spirit of Prophecy” paragraph states the ministry’s reception of her writings and their relationship to Scripture. Quoting GC/SC alone is not an independent historical authentication of a prophetic gift. The phrase “lesser light” was removed from this formulation because its source is outside the requested two books, not because the phrase was found false.

5. **Video content:** All 30 written descriptions were reviewed; nine were revised. Titles and metadata remain the existing catalog’s labels. Their accuracy, every spoken assertion, specific nutrition claims, contemporary political interpretations, and historical arguments require the recordings/transcripts and relevant evidence. In particular, a GC chapter link does not certify every Antiochus/Rome argument made in a video.

6. **Ministry operations:** Identity, speakers’ titles, contact information, ministry history, app progress, donation processing, and actual help to families require the ministry’s own confirmation. The copy presents future assistance as a vision and avoids invented prices, dates, allocations, or guaranteed results.

7. **Live site versus preview:** The source and live site contain a fuller sanctuary quiz module; the preview has a short introductory lesson. A revised module is included for later implementation. The current live `/prophecy/1844` page and the newly revised preview study are different versions; the earlier approved 1844 revision still needs to be carried into the actual website.

The sanctuary is not merely an ancient relic of Israel's history—it is a living revelation of God's plan to save humanity. When God instructed Moses to build the sanctuary, He said, "Let them make me a sanctuary; that I may dwell among them" (Exodus 25:8).
